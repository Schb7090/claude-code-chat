What it does

The planner rips and crosscuts them for 73 designs, and checks every span for your load. It also offers 100 Buy & build designs that choose their own materials and thicknesses (plywood, lumber, steel tube, pipe, blocks, crates), use your boards where the thickness fits, and give you a shopping list. Then 49 Pro / hard builds (rigged at height, tensile, welded steel, big outdoor rigs) with rigging calculations at your own safety factor. Every design has a numbered build manual.

Room and load

Span check: long-term sag under the chosen load at most 1/300 of the span, creep included. It is an engineering estimate, not a certified rating. Tables, seats and beds use their own loads (people, 100–150 kg at mid-span, 0.4–2 kN/m² on tops), stated in each design’s notes; the material you choose still sets the stiffness.
